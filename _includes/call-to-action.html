My POV

I work on a variety of video content, but there are areas where I'm most comfortable:

  • Video Games: visual dynamics, timing, responsiveness
  • Social: fast-paced formats, contemporary language, immediacy
  • Film: language, rhythm, scene construction
  • Food: aesthetics, texture, visual appeal

These are worlds I know from the inside, and they influence the way I edit. At the same time, I'm interested in exploring new areas and adapting my approach to different languages, always looking for the best way to make each project work.
